The ToF experiment showed me why a valid distance reading must be interpreted carefully. At approximately half depth, clear water produced a median distance close to the empty-cup result, so the sensor was not reliably tracking the water surface. The cardboard control provided a useful comparison because it produced a clearly defined target near the cup rim. The simulated wastewater gave more consistent readings at full level, but this result applies to the tested mixture and setup.

The load-cell experiment also changed how I interpreted “half full.” After subtracting the empty-cup mass, the half-depth measurement represented approximately 40.3% of the full volume. This helped me understand that sensor readings, container geometry, and calibration all affect the final level estimate.
