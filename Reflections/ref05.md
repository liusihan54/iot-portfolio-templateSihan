One difficulty was understanding why several strip pixels remained illuminated after sending a command for pixel 0. Each command changes only one pixel, so previously assigned colors remain until they are overwritten or cleared. Testing individual commands and then using `clear` helped me understand how the strip stores its current display state.

The current measurements also made the power requirements more concrete. At the same luminance setting, all eight pixels drew approximately 0.03 A in red and 0.09 A in white. This showed why the displayed color matters when estimating consumption. I also learned to consider the multimeter range: a small current may be below the useful resolution of the 10 A setting, so a displayed zero does not necessarily mean that no current is flowing.
